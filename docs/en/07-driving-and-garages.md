# Driving and garages without loading screens

## Driving (prototype) — `viewer/src/drive.ts`
- Arcade handling (not the game physics; that will come from the decomp: `zPhysics`, `zDynamics`).
- Ground and collisions by rays against the real geometry of nearby tiles. The BVH (acceleration structure) is built in the loader thread and arrives serialized: driving holds 60 fps.
- Keys: W/S, A/D, Space (handbrake), R (back to the road), E (garage), Esc (exit).

## Garages — `tools/src/cli/garages.ts` + `viewer/src/garage.ts`
In the original game the `menu_gate_*` zone calls `EQuitToFE(GARAGETYPE_*)`: it leaves the world and loads the menu [D]. Here the world is never left:

| Step | How |
|---|---|
| Zone | 15 zones from `GAMEPLAY.BIN` (doc 05) [V] |
| Building | Nearest of its kind: `XB_CT_SafehouseA`, `XB_CarParkDealership`, `XB_ChopShopA` (oriented box from the instance matrix) [V] |
| Real door | Building triangles using the `ARC_HOSPITAL_GARAGEDOOR` texture (all of them use it), grouped; the group closest to the zone gives centre, normal and size: 5.7–8 m × 3.3–6 m [V] |
| Preload | Within 150 m the menu interior (`FRONTEND\PLATFORMS\*.BIN`) is loaded and placed inside the building, oriented by its open side (ray analysis) |
| Press E | Shader cut-out: the door opening and the building's inner volume (with margins: outer walls, roof and floor stay). The new roller door uses the original texture and lifts with easing |
| Inside | Interior collisions; marked bay; once parked the door closes and the camera moves into a showcase orbit |
| Leaving | E opens; once you cross the threshold outwards the door closes and everything is restored |

Without an identified building (safehouses 2, 4 and 5) the door faces the respawn point [H].
