Most Wantednative on Mac

Need for Speed: Most Wanted (2005), running natively on macOS with Metal. You build it from your own PS2 ISO: the installer detects PAL (SLES-53559) or NTSC-U (SLUS-21351) and converts the game on your Mac. Open reverse-engineering project. It does not include or distribute game data. Version 0.7.2 · Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 14+.

What people ask

Frequently asked
questions

What is Rockport Native?

Rockport Native is a native Mac port of Need for Speed: Most Wanted (2005) for PlayStation 2. Its own Swift and Metal engine reads your ISO, converts the game data and runs it full screen at 60 fps, with the original physics, artificial intelligence, interface and story scripts.

Does it include the game, or do I need my own ISO?

It includes no game data. You need your own PS2 copy: the European PAL release (SLES-53559) or the American NTSC-U release (SLUS-21351). The installer detects the region by reading the disc and converts the data on your Mac in about a minute. Nothing from the game is uploaded anywhere.

Which Macs does it run on, and how much does it cost?

It runs on Apple Silicon Macs with macOS 14 or later. It is free and open source under the MIT licence; the current version is 0.7.2, downloaded as a .dmg from GitHub. You can play with a keyboard or with Xbox, PS5, PS4, PS3 and PS2 controllers over USB.

Is it a PS2 emulator?

No. It is a port: the game is rebuilt on a native engine instead of emulating the console. 99.94 % of the disc is understood, all 18,909 functions of the executable have been disassembled, and the engine runs the 258 original Lua scripts that drive the story and the races.

Will there be a Windows or mobile version?

Today there is only a Mac version, but the project is designed to reach Windows and mobile. All the reverse-engineering work (disc formats, database, physics and scripts) is platform independent and documented in Spanish and English in the repository.

What is the Rust Belt expansion?

It is a community-proposed expansion that continues the story after beating the Blacklist: four new districts next to Rockport (Millbrook, Ironwood, Calder Bay and Black Hollow), a second Blacklist and classic cars. It is designed on the game's real map, but it is not playable yet.

How can I contribute?

The repository is public on GitHub and accepts contributions through reviewed pull requests. Help is needed on the native engine (Swift and Metal), on reverse engineering formats and scripts, on original car modelling and on designing the expansion's districts.
